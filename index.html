Quizzle Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

Developer: Palmer Anderson

Contact: abovelimits119@gmail.com

This policy explains how the Quizzle Android app (com.quizzle.app) handles information. Quizzle is a trivia game for a general audience that includes children aged 6 and up. We designed it to collect as little information as possible.

1. The short version

  • Quizzle has no accounts and no sign-up, and we do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • Your game progress, settings and age are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Quizzle shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect some device and usage information to deliver ads, as explained below. Players under 13 get only child-directed, non-personalized, G-rated ads.
  • We (the developer) do not run any servers and don't receive any of your data.

2. Who can use Quizzle

Quizzle is for players aged 6 and older. The age prompt on first launch accepts ages from 6 upward.

3. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following on your device to make the game work:

  • Age: entered on first launch and changeable in Settings. It is used only on the device to (a) pick your starting difficulty, (b) choose suitable trivia categories (see section 5) and (c) choose the right ad settings (see sections 6 and 7). It is never transmitted.
  • Game progress: questions you've answered (so they aren't repeated), category stats used to build rounds, difficulty level, ranks earned and best rank.
  • Preferences: theme (Light/Dark/System) and round length.
  • A trivia session token from the Open Trivia Database, used to avoid repeated questions. It contains no personal information.
  • Your ad consent choice (users 13+ in regions where consent is required), saved by Google's User Messaging Platform.

You can delete all of this at any time by clearing the app's data (Android Settings → Apps → Quizzle → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the app.

4. Trivia questions (Open Trivia Database)

Quizzle needs an internet connection and downloads questions from the Open Trivia Database (opentdb.com). These requests ask only for questions (for example a category and difficulty) and contain no personal information. As with any website, the service may see your device's IP address when it answers a request. See opentdb.com for its practices.

5. Age-appropriate content

For players under 13, Quizzle filters out trivia categories that may contain mature themes. These players only get questions from categories suited to younger audiences.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Quizzle is free and supported by a banner ad and optional rewarded ads, both provided by Google AdMob, which takes part in Google Play's Families Self-Certified Ads SDK Program. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the advertising ID or app set ID,
  • ad interaction data (for example, ad views and taps),
  • diagnostic and performance information, and
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate (not precise) location.

Google handles this information under its own policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Optional rewarded ads (hints): during a quiz you can tap Hint and choose to watch a short ad; when it finishes, two wrong answers are removed. Rewarded ads are only shown if you ask for one, are limited to one per question and three per round, and you can always say no and keep playing. They use exactly the same age-based settings and consent rules as the banner (sections 7 and 8), and whether you used a hint is stored only on your device.

Under 13 / age unknown: ads are limited to content rated G (suitable for general audiences).

Ages 13 and up: ads may be rated up to T (Teen).

7. Children under 13 (COPPA and GDPR-K)

  • For users who enter an age under 13, and for anyone whose age isn't known yet, ad requests are marked child-directed and under the age of consent. Only non-personalized, G-rated ads are requested, which means no interest-based advertising and no remarketing.
  • Children are not shown a consent form. We never ask a child to consent to personalized advertising, and children never get personalized ads.
  • Only the child-directed setting (yes/no) goes with ad requests. The age itself is never sent.
  • We do not knowingly collect, use or disclose personal information from children. The app has no accounts, chat, user-generated content, social features or in-app purchases.
  • Under Google's child-directed treatment, information the AdMob SDK processes for child users is limited to what's needed to serve and measure ads (contextual advertising, frequency capping, fraud prevention and security).
  • Parents and guardians: we don't hold any of your child's data on any server, so there's nothing for us to look up or delete. You can remove everything the app stores by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. If you have questions or concerns, contact us at abovelimits119@gmail.com and we'll respond promptly.

8. Users 13 and older: ad consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • For users aged 13 or older, ads may be rated up to T (Teen).
  • Users aged 13 or older in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a Google-certified consent management platform. It asks whether Google may use your data for personalized ads.
  • If you consent, ads may be personalized. If you don't, you get non-personalized ads, which still use limited data for things like frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement.
  • You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options in the app.
  • Outside those regions, users 13+ may get personalized ads where the law allows. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

9. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

On-device processing of your age and progress is necessary to provide the game you've asked to use. Personalized advertising is based on your consent, which you can withdraw in Privacy options. Non-personalized and child-directed advertising, along with fraud prevention, rely on our legitimate interest in funding a free app. You have rights under the GDPR, including access, deletion, objection and the right to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we hold no personal data, most requests can be met by clearing the app's data. For requests about ad data, see Google's privacy controls linked above.

10. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the AdMob processing described in section 6, the app shares no information with third parties.

11. Security

All network traffic from the app (questions and ads) uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data stored on the device is protected by Android's app sandbox.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
